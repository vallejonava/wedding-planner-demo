Visa:

US citizens need an Indian e-Visa (~$25–80). Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in — 3–5 business days processing.

Get an eSIM via Airalo or Holafly before boarding. Download WhatsApp for the weekend.

December in coastal Karnataka: 75–90°F (24–32°C), low humidity, gentle sea breeze.

Rooms reserved at Manthan Beach Resort for all guests. No booking needed from your end!

💧
Water:

Stick to bottled water only, including for brushing teeth. Widely available and inexpensive.

What is the dress code? +

Mehendi: Relaxed, vibrant.

Are children welcome? +

What about dietary restrictions? +
Please mention restrictions in your RSVP. We'll make sure to accommodate.
Do US citizens need a visa? +
Yes — Indian e-Visa at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Processing 3–5 days; apply at least 2–4 weeks early.
Is India safe to visit? +
Yes. Mangalore and Bangalore are welcoming, family-oriented cities. US State Dept rates India Level 2: Exercise Normal Precautions.
What's the weather like in December? +
Warm sunny days 75–90°F (24–32°C), low humidity, very little rain. Evenings are beautifully cool.
